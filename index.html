turntall.com — private horizontal-to-vertical video editor

Convert landscape footage into a 9:16 vertical video without uploading the source, creating an account, or adding a watermark. turntall.com processes the video locally on your device in desktop Google Chrome, Firefox, or Safari.

Trim, reframe, and follow a moving subject

Choose exact start and end frames, preview the selected segment, keep or remove audio, and change output speed from 0.5× to 3×. A centered vertical crop is available for simple conversions. When a person or object moves, draw a box around the subject and the local tracker keeps that subject inside the portrait frame. You can add corrections after large movements or scene changes before export.

Designed for private, local video editing

The selected video stays in the browser. Analysis, crop planning, preview, encoding, and download happen on the device; there is no server upload path for source footage. The editor is free to use and the finished file does not contain a turntall.com logo or promotional watermark.
