Private, local video editing

Cut and reframe videos in your browser

GifTiger is a browser-based video cutter that processes your files locally on your device. Trim a video and download the result without uploading or sharing the original file.

Create 9:16 vertical videos from horizontal footage, select the subject you want to keep in frame, and track its movement through the clip. Manual subject positions make it easy to correct tracking or handle scene changes.

Choose precise start and end times, keep or remove audio, adjust video speed, preview the crop, and export a social-media-ready vertical video—all inside Google Chrome.
